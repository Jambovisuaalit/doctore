# League differences

- KBO and NPB run environments, roster rules, ballparks, schedules, and tie rules differ materially.
- Do not reuse MLB, KBO, or NPB calibration interchangeably.
- Verify sportsbook settlement for ties and shortened games.
- Doubleheaders, rain, and starter announcements may be less synchronized across sources; preserve timestamps and conflicts.
- KBO bullpen workload must be reconstructed from KBO pitcher-level records. MLB-derived fatigue thresholds are research priors only and require KBO-specific chronological validation.
- Bullpen stress applies to full-game markets and must not be transferred unchanged to first-five markets.
